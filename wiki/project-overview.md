## What it is

Frictionless Architect is the working name for a **Frictionless Architecture &
Governance Platform** — a system that keeps an organisation's architecture as a
live, machine-readable model and automates governance on top of it (FR-001),
including automated enforcement of APRA **CPS 230** (operational resilience) and
**CPS 234** (information security) (FR-020)
(`specs/001-governance-platform/spec.md` §"Functional Requirements").

`README.md` frames it more narrowly as the repo stands today: "a platform for
describing, validating, and visualising enterprise architecture models
(ArchiMate) backed by a graph database". It names spec 001 as the business
specification where "the full platform requirements live", and `ARCHITECTURE.md`
as the target repository topology (`README.md` §intro).

The README's badge row gives the repo's vital signs: licensed **AGPL-3.0**,
**Python 3.11+**, test runs on the GitHub Actions `ci.yml` workflow,
coverage reported to Codecov, and a SonarCloud quality gate that also tracks
bugs, security rating and maintainability (`README.md` §intro badges). See
[Development & Quickstart](development.md) for the gates behind those signals.

## Users

The spec names these personas (`specs/001-governance-platform/spec.md`
§"User Scenarios & Testing"):

| Persona | What they do |
|---|---|
| Developer | Runs compliance checks, requests Break-Glass exceptions (P1); initiates and progresses the specification flow, with AI-drafted ADRs (P2) |
| Architect | Reviews AI-drafted ADRs and applies a verifiable cryptographic attestation (P1) |
| Compliance Officer | Asks natural-language questions of the Regulatory Traceability Matrix and receives compliance evidence (P1) |
| System Administrator / Platform Engineer | Monitors platform health, security and the governance KPIs (P3) |

## Headline capabilities

From `specs/001-governance-platform/spec.md` §"Functional Requirements":

- **Semantic model / digital twin** — technical components mapped to data
  domains, Critical Business Services (CBS) with impact tolerances, and
  regulations (FR-001, FR-002, FR-013), fed by ingested interface and
  data-model descriptions (FR-012).
- **Decision capture** — AI-assisted ADR drafting from unstructured text, with
  sensitive data removed before external processing, human cryptographic
  attestation, and conflict detection against existing ADRs (FR-003–FR-006,
  FR-014).
- **Drift & emergency management** — drift monitoring (FR-007), a Break-Glass
  protocol for intentional, time-bounded drift in P1 incidents (FR-008),
  "Managed Drift" technical-debt records (FR-009), and remediation proposals
  that return a deployment to its as-designed state (FR-021).
- **Audit** — a queryable Regulatory Traceability Matrix (FR-010),
  natural-language compliance queries (FR-011), and one-action compliance-pack
  exports (FR-023).
- **Machine-readable, self-documenting artefacts** — every artefact stays
  machine-readable (FR-019), and diagrams and documentation are generated from
  the model (FR-022).

The headline outcomes are provisional benchmarks: Mean Time to Compliance down
30%, drift-detection accuracy above 98%, Governance Friction Coefficient down
25%, and traceability-matrix queries within 1 minute
(`specs/001-governance-platform/spec.md` §"Success Criteria").

## How the platform is drawn and decomposed

The platform decomposes into the **six subsystems** of ADR-0011, modelled in
`architecture/model/`, from which the C4 and ArchiMate views are generated — see
[Architecture Overview](architecture.md) and
[Architecture Model: Ecosystem](architecture-model-ecosystem.md). The retired
specification's modelling-IDE feature inventory and UI overview are preserved as
unscoped backlog in GH #59, not as requirements.

## What is built today

Only one narrow slice is built: the **Neo4j schema visualiser** — a FastAPI
service that aggregates an ArchiMate schema from a live Neo4j instance and/or a
bundled sample model, falling back to `sample-data/` when Neo4j is unreachable
(`README.md` §"What is built today"). See
[Visualizer Service](visualizer-service.md) and
[Platform Specification & API](platform-spec.md) (spec 002).

Not covered by these sources: the MVP scope (single-user, locally run) is
recorded in ADR-0024 and discussed on [Architecture Overview](architecture.md).
