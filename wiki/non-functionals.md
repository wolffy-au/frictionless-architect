`NONFUNCTIONALS.md` holds the abstracted, high-level quality-attribute
requirements derived from the feature specification. It is one of the four
"deeper detail" documents the [Governance & Constitution](governance-and-constitution.md)
points to, alongside `TECHNICAL.md` and the feature specs.

All entries below are direct from `NONFUNCTIONALS.md` §"Categories".

## Performance

- Critical queries under **1 minute**; common API queries **< 1s**; complex
  queries **< 5s**.
- System design should reduce key compliance- and user-effort metrics.

(These align with `specs/001-governance-platform/spec.md` SC-006/SC-007 — see
[Platform Specification & API](platform-spec.md).)

## Security

- Sensitive data masked or anonymized before processing by external or
  high-risk components.
- Critical artifacts/decisions undergo human review with verifiable
  cryptographic attestation.
- Authentication via industry-standard mechanisms (secure tokens); authorization
  enforced by policy (**ABAC** named as the example).
- Data encrypted **at rest and in transit**.
- The platform's **own deployment** undergoes regular automated security
  scanning. This was
  formerly functional requirement FR-018 in spec 001 and now lives here as an
  operational requirement; threat modelling of *client* architectures is a
  library output, not part of it (`NONFUNCTIONALS.md` §Security → "Security
  Assessments"; see [Platform Specification & API](platform-spec.md)).
- Compliance with relevant information-security standards and regulations.

## Reliability & Availability

- Real-time monitoring of key operational parameters and deviations.
- Controlled, auditable exceptions/overrides for critical operations (the
  Break-Glass mechanism).
- Regular automated health and configuration-consistency checks.
- Automated **resilience testing** (e.g. chaos engineering) that proves the
  as-designed recovery logic works in simulated outages, in support of APRA
  CPS 230 — carried over from the retired `PROJECT_SPECIFICATION.md`
  (`NONFUNCTIONALS.md` §"Reliability & Availability" → "Resilience Testing").
- Standardized, machine-readable API error reporting.
- Compliance with operational-resilience standards.

## Usability & Efficiency

- Minimize user effort and friction for governance tasks.
- Support natural-language querying for complex datasets where appropriate.
- All artifacts in machine-readable formats.

## Compliance

- Automate enforcement of compliance policies and regulatory standards.
- Verifiable human attestation for critical decisions.

## Code Quality & Maintainability

- Readable, maintainable code to defined standards; documented public
  interfaces kept current; modular, reusable components; RESTful, versioned APIs.

## Testing & Quality Assurance

- Test coverage must exceed a defined threshold — **> 90%**
  (`NONFUNCTIONALS.md` §"Testing & Quality Assurance"; `TECHNICAL.md` and
  Constitution II say 100% on core business logic).

## Process & Automation

- CI/CD for build/test/deploy; version control with a clear commit strategy.

## Error Management & Observability

- Standardized error responses and robust exception handling.
- Observability via structured logging, metrics collection, and distributed
  tracing (detailed in `TECHNICAL.md` §"Observability and Logging").
